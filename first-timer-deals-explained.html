Getting started

How it works

Three steps, no coupon, no code, no sign-up form. If it is your first time shopping with us, the deal is already waiting for you.

  1. 1

    Come in, or order ahead

    Walk into the shop at 8717 Van Nuys Blvd in Panorama City, across from Panorama Mall with free lot parking, or order ahead on our Weedmaps store and pick up in store.

  2. 2

    Tell the budtender you are a first-timer

    That is all it takes. Let them know it is your first visit and they will apply the deal to your order at the register.

  3. 3

    Pick your dollar item

    Choose one with your order: the indoor eighth, the 1g vape, or the 100mg edible, for $1. Then do it again on your second and third orders, and try a different one each time if you like.

Making the call

Which one should you pick?

There is no wrong answer, but each one suits a different kind of smoker. Match the item to how you already enjoy cannabis:

Flower

Pick the eighth if you smoke flower

A full 3.5g indoor eighth for a dollar is the biggest bag of the three. If you roll, pack a bowl, or just want to see what our indoor flower is about, start here.

Vape

Pick the vape if you want discreet and ready to go

The 1g vape needs no grinder, no lighter, and no setup. It is pocket-sized, low-key, and ready the second you are.

Edible

Pick the edible if you prefer not to smoke

The 100mg edible skips the smoke entirely. New to edibles? Start with a small piece, wait, and see how you feel before having more.

Still torn between formats? Our guide on how to pick between flower, a vape, an edible, or a pre-roll walks through how each one feels, how long it lasts, and who it suits best.

Before you check out

The fine print

Short version: it is a genuinely good deal, with a few simple rules so it stays fair for every first-timer.

  • A minimum purchase applies to each order with a dollar item.
  • One deal item per order.
  • The deal runs on your first three orders only.
  • Deals cannot always be combined with other offers, and the register has the final word at checkout.
  • Terms match the deal as listed on the shop's Weedmaps listing, so what you see there is what you get here.

Quick questions

First-timer deal FAQ

Do I need a coupon or code?+

No. Just tell a budtender it is your first visit and they will apply the deal at the register.

Can I get all three dollar items on one order?+

One per order, spread across your first three orders, so you can try all three: the eighth on visit one, the vape on visit two, and the edible on visit three, in any order you like.

I am a medical patient, does the deal apply to me?+

The deal is for first-time customers. Medical patients can ask a budtender how it applies to their order, and the team will walk you through it at the counter.
