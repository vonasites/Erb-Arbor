First Visit · Panorama City

First time in a dispensary? Here is the whole thing.

Published October 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Walking into a licensed dispensary for the first time feels more like checking into a nice hotel than anything else: a quick ID check, a friendly person behind the counter, and a menu you can take your time with. Here is exactly how a first visit works at Erb & Arbor in Panorama City, step by step, so there are no surprises.

Short Answer

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID and be 21 or older, or 18 or older with a valid medical recommendation. Expect a quick ID check at the door, a budtender who walks you through the menu at your pace, and checkout with a debit or credit card or cash.

01

Before you go

California keeps the rules simple. Adults 21 and older can buy cannabis recreationally with nothing more than a valid ID. Patients 18 and older can shop with a valid medical recommendation from a licensed physician, or a state-issued Medical Marijuana Identification Card (MMIC). Nobody under those ages can be on the sales floor, so leave the kids and anyone under 21 without a medical recommendation at home for this trip.

What counts as a valid ID

  • A state driver's license or state ID card, from California or any other state
  • A US passport or passport card
  • A foreign passport
  • A US military ID

The one rule that matters: it has to be government issued, show your photo and date of birth, and be unexpired. An expired license will not work, even if it is really you, so check the date before you drive over.

If you are a medical patient

Bring your recommendation letter or MMIC card along with your photo ID. Staff verify it at check-in, and your purchases get medical patient tax treatment where it applies.

How you can pay

Erb & Arbor takes debit cards, credit cards, and cash, so pay whichever way is easiest. No need to stop at an ATM first.

Getting Here

Erb & Arbor is at 8717 Van Nuys Blvd in Panorama City, right across from Panorama Mall, with free parking in the lot. Hours are Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 9:55 PM, and Saturday to Sunday, 9:00 AM to 9:55 PM. Questions before you come? Call (866) 272-6799.

02

At the door

When you walk in, a staff member greets you and checks your ID. It takes a few seconds: they confirm it is valid, confirm your age, and wave you in. First-timers sometimes worry this step means paperwork or a wait. It does not. There is no form to fill out, no membership to join, and no purchase required to come inside.

Everyone gets checked, every time. California law requires it for every customer on every visit, whether you are 22 or 62, and whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth. It is the same rule at every licensed shop in the state, so do not take it personally when the regular in front of you gets carded too.

The tone at Erb & Arbor is calm on purpose. A lot of people walking in are first-timers, caregivers picking up for a patient, or folks who have not bought cannabis since it came in a baggie. The house rule is simple: there are no dumb questions. If you are unsure about anything, ask at the door and someone will point you the right way.

03

At the counter

Once you are in, a budtender helps you one on one. Think of them like a good bartender or a pharmacist for the menu: their whole job is matching you to the right product, and they would rather spend five minutes getting it right than sell you the wrong thing fast.

The conversation usually covers three things:

  • What you want to feel. Relaxed and sleepy, clear-headed and social, pain relief, help focusing. There is no wrong answer, and "I have no idea" is a perfectly good starting point.
  • Which format fits your life. Flower you smoke, a ready-to-go pre-roll, a discreet vape, or an edible. Each hits differently: smoked and vaped products are felt within minutes, while edibles can take 30 minutes to 2 hours to kick in and last much longer.
  • Your budget. Tell them a number and they will work inside it. Good shops stock solid options at every price, and Erb & Arbor runs first-timer and daily deals worth asking about.

One piece of advice every budtender will give you: start low and go slow. A smaller dose of a milder product is a much better first experience than too much of a strong one. You can always have more another day.

And if you do not want the full guided tour, that is fine too. You are welcome to take your time and just browse the cases, read the labels, and wave a budtender over only when something catches your eye.

04

Ordering ahead

Prefer to decide at home? You can. Browse the menu on the Erb & Arbor site and build a pickup list of what you want, at your own pace, with no one waiting on you. When your list looks right, finish the order on the shop's Weedmaps store, and it will be packed and waiting at the counter.

You pay at pickup, by debit card, credit card, or cash, and you will still need to show your ID when you collect it. Ordering ahead is the fastest route in and out, and it is a great first visit if you would rather walk in already knowing what you are getting.

05

Taking it home

Everything you buy leaves the shop sealed in child-resistant, exit packaging. Keep it sealed until you get home. On the drive, put it in the trunk or a locked container, out of the passenger cabin and out of reach, the same way you would transport alcohol. Open containers do not belong in the car.

  • Never drive after consuming. If you try something and feel it, you are done driving for the day. Full stop.
  • Keep it away from children and pets. Edibles can look exactly like ordinary candy or gummies. Store them somewhere a kid or a dog cannot get to, ideally locked.
  • Store it like you mean it. At home, keep products in their original labeled packaging, in a cool, dark spot, treated like medicine or liquor: put away, not left out.

That is the whole visit. ID at the door, a conversation at the counter, a sealed bag in the trunk. Most first-timers leave wondering what they were nervous about.

Quick questions

Do I need a medical card to shop at Erb & Arbor?

No. Adults 21 and older can shop recreationally with just a valid photo ID. A medical card or recommendation is only needed for patients aged 18 to 20, and for medical patient tax treatment.

Can I pay with a credit card?

Yes. Erb & Arbor accepts debit cards and credit cards, and cash works too.

Can I just come in and look around?

Yes. Browsing is completely fine, there is no pressure to buy, and the budtenders are happy to answer questions while you look.
