How to Pick · The Basics

Flower, vape, edible, or pre-roll? How to pick.

Published October 21, 2026 · 8 min read

The Short Version

Short answer

The formats differ mainly in how fast they start and how long they last. Anything smoked or vaped starts in minutes and lasts about 1 to 3 hours. Edibles take 30 minutes to 2 hours to start and last 4 to 8 hours, so beginners should start with a low dose and wait. Pick the format that matches your plans, then start smaller than you think you need to.

Flower

Flower is the classic format: dried cannabis buds that you grind and smoke in a pipe, a bong, or papers you roll yourself. It gives you full control over how much you use, hit by hit, and the widest choice of strains, aromas, and price points. The trade off is the setup: you need a pipe or papers, a grinder helps, and it smells like cannabis while you use it.

Pre-rolls

A pre-roll is a joint that is already rolled for you: ground flower in paper, ready to light. There is nothing to grind, roll, or clean up, which makes it the easiest first buy and the most shareable format. Quality varies more than with loose flower, so it pays to buy from brands that use whole flower rather than trim.

Vapes

A vape heats cannabis oil in a cartridge or pod until it turns to vapor, no flame and no rolling. Vapes are discreet, with far less smell than smoking, and they hit fast and potent, so the standard advice is small hits: take one, wait a few minutes, then decide if you want another. Pods and cartridges need a matching battery, while disposables work straight out of the package.

Edibles

Edibles are food and drinks infused with cannabis: gummies, chocolates, mints, and beverages. Nothing to smoke and nothing to smell, and they are long lasting and strong per dollar. The catch is patience. An edible has to be digested before it works, so start with 2.5 to 5 mg of THC and wait a full 2 hours before taking more. Taking a second dose too early is the most common beginner mistake there is.

Concentrates

Concentrates are cannabis reduced to its most potent form: wax, live resin, rosin, and similar extracts, usually dabbed or vaped. They are for experienced customers. A concentrate can be several times stronger than flower, and a little goes a long way, often a piece the size of a grain of rice. If you are new, this is a format to grow into later, with flower, pre-rolls, vapes, and low dose edibles first.

Frequently asked questions

Why do edibles feel stronger than smoking?

When you eat THC, your liver converts it into a different form, 11-hydroxy-THC, that is stronger and lasts longer than the THC you inhale. That conversion is why a small edible dose can feel bigger than the same amount smoked, and why it takes so much longer to fade.

How much should a beginner start with?

For edibles, start with 2.5 to 5 mg of THC and wait the full 2 hours before taking more. For flower or a vape, take one or two small hits, then wait 15 minutes to see how you feel before having any more.

Can I mix formats?

You can, but it is smarter not to at first. Pick one format at a time until you know how each one feels on its own. Mixing makes it hard to tell what caused what, and the effects stack up faster than people expect.
